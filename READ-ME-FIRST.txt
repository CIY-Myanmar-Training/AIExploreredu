AI EXPLORER — 6 AI mission games
CIY Club Myanmar × ScopeIT Education · Created by Coach Penny
=========================================================

SETTING UP A LAPTOP (5 minutes)
1. Copy the whole "ai-explorer" folder onto the laptop. Keep all files together.
2. Open the folder and double-click  index.html  (use Google Chrome).
3. Click the game you want to use. Press F11 for full screen.
4. No internet needed. Everything runs from the folder.

POSE PILOT (camera booth)
- The first time, Chrome asks to use the camera. Click "Allow".
- If the camera doesn't start: click the camera icon in the address bar,
  choose "Always allow", then reload the page.
- Best results: plain wall behind the child, good light, child stands
  1–2 steps back so arms are visible.

TRAIN MY OWN AI (camera booth)
- The child picks one of 6 missions (match the mission card):
  1 Thumbs up or down  2 Happy or sad  3 Awake or asleep
  4 Rock, paper, scissors  5 Is anyone there?  6 My own things
- Plain background, good light, hold hands or things close to the camera.
- After winning, the child can print a certificate (needs a printer;
  otherwise take a photo of the screen).
- Camera pictures stay on the laptop. Nothing is saved or sent.

THINK QUESTION
Every game ends with one question. The child must answer it before the
secret code appears. A wrong answer shows a hint.

SECRET CODES (volunteers only — don't print on child materials)
  1 Pose Pilot ............ SKYBIRD
  2 Trash Island Trainer .. RECYCLE
  3 Word Machine .......... NEXTWORD
  4 Catch the Liar Bot .... FACTCHECK
  5 Stranger in the Chat .. SAFEHERO
  6 Train My Own AI ....... BUILDER
To change them, edit the CODES list at the top of assets/common.js.

BETWEEN CHILDREN
Press "Next player" on the win screen. If a child walks away mid-game,
press F5 to restart.

LANGUAGE
English is the default. The switch at the top right changes the older
games to Burmese, and the laptop remembers the choice.

MARKETING LINE
The menu and every win screen show a short "learn with CIY Club Myanmar &
ScopeIT Education" message. To change the contact line, edit BRAND at the
top of assets/common.js.
